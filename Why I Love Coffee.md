# I really, really love coffee
## When did this love affair begin?
###### Continue reading to learn all about it!

## ORIGIN STORY

*The year was 2009*  
_I was a poor college student needing some extra cash to fund my not-so luxurious lifestyle_

**THEN I SAW THE STARBUCKS JOB ADVERTISEMENT**  
__My excitement level was high__

_But there was only one **tiny** problem_

**I didn't actually like coffee!**  
*How was that going to work?*  


## It was time to make a Pros/Cons list


| Pros  | Cons |
| ------------- |:-------------:|
| Extra cash      | Would I miss class due to my shifts?     |
| New friends?      | Would working cut into my study time?    |
| Potential unlimited energy source on tap | Oh yeah... I still don't like coffee     |


### Extra thoughts

* I had heard that Starbucks partners (employees) were awarded one free beverage per shift. That's a nice perk, especially considering the price of their drinks!
    * (Then I remembered I don't like coffee)
    * (But then I thought, maybe this was a growth opportunity?)
    * (Also, don't they have tea?)

### I decided to go for it, and compiled my Mission Objectives

1. Dust off (read: CREATE) a resumé
2. Ask my great aunt Lisa to be my reference
3. Submit my application in person at the café
    1. This gives a nice personal touch
    2. Also I can scope out the potential workplace
4. Sit back, try to relax, and wait for the call!

## Appearances can be deceiving - Behind this cheery smile is a cup of hot mud!

![Aforementioned cup of hot mud.](https://i.dailymail.co.uk/i/pix/2015/03/09/0C07226D00000578-0-image-a-23_1425939890281.jpg "This is a the cup of hot mud.")

## Fast Pass to the Conclusion of this Tale

**I got the job! And I learned to love coffee over time.**  
*Side note: I did end up missing a lot of class and study time, but I graduated eventually after an extra year of studies (and many, **MANY**, caffeinated beverages.)*
